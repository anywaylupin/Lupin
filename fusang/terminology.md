# Terminology of Fusang

Names inside Fusang are pinyin and never contain zh, ch, th or tr; Shangri-La and Chang'e are kept as chosen, the only exceptions.
Names from beyond Fusang are Western words, such as Dayside and Nightside.
Gods' names belong to places, houses and machines, never to a single person, so Xihe is a house, Kunlun a tower and Chang'e a dome.
Hou Yi is the one person this record names: a human, a Kuafu, called the Archer.
Fusang calls the seed Yin (阴) and the antiseed Yang (阳).
The dead star is Xukai, a name without characters, and the planet has no name yet.
Characters appear only where the record gives them, and the ten Heavenly Stems carry their own.
Years count from the star's death, Fusang dates everything after Yiwan from Yiwan, and "always" marks what stood before the count began.
