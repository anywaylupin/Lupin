# Terminology of Fusang

Names inside Fusang are pinyin and never contain the letter pairs zh, ch, th or tr; the only exceptions are Shangri-La and Chang'e, kept as chosen.
Names beyond Fusang are Western words, as are the planet-wide terms Dayside and Nightside.
Gods' names belong to places, houses and machines, never to a single person, so Xihe names a house and Chang'e a dome.
Hou Yi is a human, a Kuafu, called the Archer.
Fusang calls the seed Yin (阴) and the antiseed Yang (阳).
The dead star is Xukai, a name with no characters, and the planet has no name yet.

First appears gives the year, counted from the star's death, when a term first appears in the history.
Before 0 means the thing already existed when the star died, and a blank means the bible gives no date.
