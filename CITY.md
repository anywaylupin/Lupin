# Fusang

The story bible for the world behind the hex sheet: one continent, Fusang, and the war between Xihe and ShenYi.
It replaces the earlier city designs.
The code in `src/hive/city/` still follows the first design and will be rebuilt once the world is settled.

## Naming

- Names inside Fusang are pinyin, avoiding zh, ch, th and tr.
- Names beyond Fusang are Western words.
- Shangri-La and Chang'e are kept as chosen.
- Fusang calls the seed **Yin** (阴) and the Yang **Yang** (阳).
- Gods' names belong to places, houses and machines, never to a single person.

## The world

- A planet like Earth in another galaxy, not yet named.
- Its star died 10,000 years ago and is now a cold, dim remnant that Fusang calls **Xukai**; the planet still circles it.
- At the moment of death the planet was more advanced than Earth today: orbital stations, fusion grids, AI and a planetary network.
- The death came as a radiation burst that killed every grid, then a storm of meteors.
- The meteors only struck the hemisphere facing the star, the **Dayside**; each Dayside continent received its own share of seeds and built its own civilisation around them.
- The **Nightside** received nothing and froze.
- Fusang is one Dayside continent, the Asia of this planet.
- The other continents talk to each other and some are as powerful as Xihe; their stories come later.

## Yunjin

Fusang calls the meteor matter **Yunjin** (陨金, fallen gold). It comes in three parts.

| Part                        | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                  | What is left                                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Yin** (阴), the seeds     | Yunjin's burning core: ten small suns. Whole, a Yin seed never runs down                                                                                                                                    | One burning; nine consumed                                                                    |
| **Yang** (阳), the antiseed | The seed's counterpart. Yin and Yang consume each other, and the reaction leaves a burnt husk and a new, weaker Yin. Inert and toxic in any normal process, and it only reacts to raw, unfiltered Yin light | All of it became the ten arrows, and firing an arrow uses it up. Only the tenth arrow is left |
| Common Yunjin               | The bulk of the meteors: the material Shangri-La, Kunlun, the carriages and every advanced machine are built from                                                                                           | Plenty, almost all held by Xihe                                                               |

**The secret:** the ten original seeds shine forever. The weaker seeds born from the Yang reaction do not.
Xihe's scientists have measured the last seed for thousands of years and found its power has not dropped at all; the Busi drew the bold conclusion, and Xihe keeps it hidden.
Eternal only means it never runs down on its own: the Yang can still consume it, which is why Xihe fears the tenth arrow as much as ever.
Everyone else on Fusang fears the last seed is dying, and every faction studies it.
Xihe keeps the fear alive on purpose: it tells the Kuafu their labour is what keeps the seed burning, so every shift in the mines feels like keeping the sun alive.

## The conflict

- **ShenYi want to free the Kuafu and bring down Xihe and Yao.** To the Kuafu they are the only people who ever brought warmth; to the Xian they are terrorists.
- **The tenth arrow** is the only thing left that can break the last seed. Fired with the Red Bow, it would consume the last seed and leave a husk and one more weak seed. Shangri-La would lose its eternal sun forever.
- **ShenYi want to hold the arrow, not fire it.** With both arrow and bow they hold the sun hostage and can force Xihe to free the Kuafu. Only the Bow Sect's fanatics truly want the shot.
- **Neither side can fire alone:** Yao has the arrow, carried by Zaoya, and ShenYi have the bow.
- **Why Xihe tolerates Yao:** only Yao knows where the arrow is, and Yao keeps a dead man's switch: a sleeper agent inside the Bow Sect, whose name no one knows, under orders to hand the arrow to ShenYi if Yao falls. Even after Yiwan, Xihe has not moved against Yao.
- **Xihe's propaganda:** the Archer froze your grandparents. It is true, and it keeps the Ring from ever fully siding with ShenYi.
- **Why Yao and ShenYi never ally:** ShenYi will never trust the house that betrayed the Archer, and Yao fears ShenYi would fire and destroy its home.
- **Racism:** the Xian look down on Yao as a lesser people, and Yao looks down on the Kuafu. Each layer has someone beneath it.
- **Yutu:** the battery is ShenYi's real reason to reach the seed. Charging it would keep Chang'e alive without firing the arrow at all.
- **Yao's lie:** Yao has always known Chang'e is on the ground and told Xihe it orbits the sky. Chang'e's power also lifts it, and its signature reads like something in the air, so the lie held. Only the head of Yao knows the truth, passed from head to head. A living ShenYi keeps Yao needed, so Yao's hunts take the expendable and miss the core: someone has to run the hunts, and someone has to be the threat that makes Yao's arrow matter.
- **Xihe's blind spot, until now:** for seven thousand years it searched the sky for Chang'e. In Yiwan it finally looks at the ground, and starts to wonder who told it to look up.
- **Charging the Sanzuwu** is rationed and argued over inside Xihe, because the public still believes the seed is dying.

## The Sanzuwu

The nine burnt husks rebuilt as machines: obedient, ruthless, three-legged, built around circles and spheres, and charged by the last seed at Kunlun's crown.
They work in three triads of three; two triads are always active and one is charging, so six are out and three are home.
Each triad holds one machine for material exploitation, one for war and one for scouting, so every role is always covered.

| Triad | Exploitation                             | War                                                | Scouting                                    |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| One   | **Yuhui** (余晖): tripod mining crawler  | **Yan** (炎): furnace-chested ground mech, offence | **Rishi** (日食): eclipse disc, stealth     |
| Two   | **Rigui** (日晷): gnomon deep-driller    | **Heizi** (黑子): one-eyed walker, defence         | **Ri'er** (日珥): crescent gunship, fast    |
| Three | **Liming** (黎明): mobile refinery tower | **Rimian** (日冕): corona flagship, command        | **Jin** (烬): ember sphere that tracks heat |

## Night in the Ring

Ten thousand years of freezing nights shaped Guanghuan, and its people are restless, divided and always one cold night from riot.

- **Kang towers** (炕): stacked homes over heat pipes stolen from the dome.
- **Raised platforms**: homes and streets lifted off the ice on Yunjin built from scraps of the same lifting power that keeps Chang'e aloft.
- **Ember cells**: light cells stolen by ShenYi, worth killing for.
- **ShenYi in the shifts**: many ShenYi work the day shift like any Kuafu, which is how they hide.
- **The night bell**: the warning before Xihe pulls the light back.
